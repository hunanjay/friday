## Relationships deserve more than memory and spreadsheets

The number of important people in your life keeps growing, while the details that matter slip away: what someone is working on, where you left off, when to follow up, and how to start a thoughtful conversation. A traditional address book only stores names and phone numbers. A traditional CRM asks you to keep filling out forms until it becomes a database no one updates.

Friday is a self-hostable AI workspace built around your **relationship brain**. It brings email, calendar, contacts, notes, and everyday conversations into one place, so context can accumulate naturally and the next right action becomes clearer.

> **Project status: Alpha.** Friday is moving quickly and is best suited to early users who want to help shape the product. Before connecting a production account, review the requested permissions and assess your own data-handling requirements.

## What Friday helps you do

### Remember the relationship, not just the contact details

Connect your Outlook contacts, or paste in chat history, meeting notes, and quick thoughts. Friday organizes the useful details into contact profiles, tags, and timelines. You can review, edit, or delete this information whenever you like—you stay in control.

### Find the right person and context with one question

Instead of searching through email, messages, and notes, ask Friday:

- “Find people in healthcare who are currently fundraising.”
- “What did I discuss with Wang last time?”
- “Who should I meet while I’m in Beijing next week?”

Friday retrieves relevant signals from your work and relationship memory, so you can get up to speed before you act.

### Turn everyday communication into the next step

Work through email, calendars, notes, and tasks in one workspace. Ask Friday to draft a reply, prepare meeting talking points, capture an idea, or organize an item on your schedule. For meaningful actions such as sending an email or changing a calendar event, Friday shows you what will happen and waits for your approval.

### Capture information in the way you already work

Friday does not require a new data-entry habit. Email exchanges, contact sync, chat content, and notes can all become sources of relationship memory. The goal is not to collect more fields—it is to have useful, trustworthy context when you need it.

## A typical flow

1. Connect your mailbox and contacts, or import an existing chat history.
2. Friday builds a baseline profile and organizes key facts and interaction context.
3. Before a meeting, follow-up, or reply, ask naturally to search, understand context, and draft the next step.
4. Review and approve important actions, keeping communication efficient and firmly under your control.

## Who it is for

- **Sales and business development professionals** who want to recall client context, progress, and relevant talking points before every follow-up.
- **Investors and founders** who need to connect scattered relationship signals and avoid missing meaningful opportunities.
- **Leaders and executives** who manage a high volume of meetings and conversations without losing the thread.
- **Knowledge workers who value long-term relationships** and want to turn scattered interactions into searchable, lasting memory.

## Get started

If you would like to shape the product, contribute an improvement, or report an issue, please see [Contributing](./CONTRIBUTING.md), the [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md), or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hunanjay/friday/issues).

## Privacy and control

Relationship data is personal. Friday follows a simple principle: you choose which data to connect and which actions are allowed. The project supports self-hosting, and asks for your confirmation before meaningful actions such as sending email or changing calendar events. Review the permissions of connected services and your deployment configuration before use.
